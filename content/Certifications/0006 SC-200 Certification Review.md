## Introduction

At my current organization I'm configuring and setting up Microsoft Defender XDR because we are transitioning away from Cortex XDR. To ensure I'm configuring and setting up Microsoft Defender XDR properly, I decided to take the [Security Operation Analyst Associate (SC-200)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/users/husenjan/credentials/1ddb60c27ac1f814) certification from Microsoft.

In this article I'll go through step-by-step guide to hopefully help you with obtaining the certification...

## Motivation, Discepline and Stress

When you read through the blog post it might seem as I'm gifted or special but in reality I'm a regular person who experience up and downs. An example this month has been extremely stressful for me because our water heater tank had to be replaced and the cost for that is $5000  USD. Instead of letting the financial burden control me I decided to continue pushing myself and stay motivated and disceplined.

## Preperation

As always I recommend reading through [Microsoft Learn: Security Operation Analysts Associate](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/security-operations-analyst/?practice-assessment-type=certification) and take notes while going through the course materials because there is a-lot of things to learn about Microsoft Sentinel and Microsoft Defender XDR. Here are some things the course materials will go through:

* Microsoft Defender XDR
    * Microsoft Defender for Endpoint
    * Microsoft Defender for Identity
    * Microsoft Defender for Cloud
    * Microsoft Defender for Cloud Apps
    * Microsoft Defender for APIs
    * Microsoft Defender for DevOPS
    * Attack Surface Reduction Rule
    * Deep Analysis
    * Advanced Hunting
    * Live Response
* Microsoft Security Copilot 
    * Investigating with AI
    * Create Promptbook
* Microsoft Purview
    * Data Loss Prevention
    * Insider Risk Management
* Microsoft Sentinel
    * Kusto Query Langauge
    * Watchlists
    * Workbooks
    * Playbooks
    * Logic Apps

After completing the course materials, I implemented the features that were recommended by Microsoft which gave me hands on experience. At my free time I spent time researching the different features using OpenAI to further increase my understanding and knowledge about different features. I know many people are against using AI but instead of going against it use it for your advantage to increase your understanding.

## Exam 

Today at 12:15 PM, I decided to check out PersonVue to see if there were any seats available and saw that a seat was available at 13:00 PM. I instantly purchased the seat and after relaxing and clearing my mind for 20 minutes I jumped into the exam.

The case studies is always the easiest and the most fun part of the exam for me. However, many of the multiple of choice questions and the multiple of choice with no way of going back and forth are the most difficult ones because the way Microsoft writes their questions is meant to confuse you between two answers. 

After completing the case studies, multiple of choice questions, and the non-revertable multiple of choice questions I decided to review the answers I selected. After completing the review I delivered in the exam and saw a passing score of 883 which means I successfully obtained the [Security Operation Analysts Associate (SC-200)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/users/husenjan/credentials/1ddb60c27ac1f814) certification.
